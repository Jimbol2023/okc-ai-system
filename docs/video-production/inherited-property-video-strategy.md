# Inherited Property Video Production Strategy

## Purpose

Create a 3-5 minute trust-first educational video for J Capital Property Group.

Funnel path:

Video -> Education Center -> Resource Guide -> Property Discussion Form

The video should build authority, SEO value, YouTube retention, website engagement, and trust. It should not feel like an ad.

## Brand Requirements

- Company: J Capital Property Group
- Logo: JC Shield Logo v1.0
- Style: trust-first, professional, modern, calm, educational
- Market: Oklahoma property-owner education
- Colors: deep navy blue, white, light gray, charcoal gray, subtle gold accent
- Fonts: Cinzel for titles and headings; clean sans-serif for body text

Logo rules:

- Use the official JC Shield Logo v1.0 on the title slide and closing slide.
- Place the logo subtly in the footer or corner of content slides.
- Maintain consistent logo sizing throughout the presentation.
- Do not distort, stretch, recolor, crop, trace, or recreate the logo.
- Preserve clear spacing around the logo.
- Use the logo as a trust and brand recognition element, not as a dominant design feature.

## Slide Plan

1. Title slide
   - Understanding Inherited Property in Oklahoma
   - A calm educational guide for organizing questions before making decisions
   - J Capital Property Group

2. What inherited property means
   - Inherited property can involve ownership, documents, family communication, timing, and property responsibilities.
   - This video is general education only.

3. Common ownership situations
   - Inherited homes may involve one owner, multiple heirs, a trust, an estate, or unclear decision authority.
   - Start by identifying who can make decisions.

4. Probate and trust considerations
   - Some situations may require probate, trust review, title review, or professional guidance.
   - Legal and title questions should be reviewed with qualified professionals.

5. Common responsibilities
   - Families often need to think through utilities, insurance, access, repairs, personal property, taxes, and security.

6. Challenges families face
   - Common challenges include distance, emotional stress, deferred maintenance, unclear paperwork, family disagreement, and timing pressure.

7. Options available
   - Possible paths may include keeping the property, renting it, repairing it, listing it, transferring ownership, or evaluating available ownership and disposition options.
   - The right path depends on the situation.

8. Key takeaways
   - Organize documents.
   - Confirm decision authority.
   - Separate facts from assumptions.
   - Understand property condition.
   - Get qualified guidance when needed.

9. Closing slide
   - Continue Learning
   - Learn more at jcapitalpropertygroup.com/resources/education
   - J Capital Property Group

## CTA Rules

Spoken primary CTA:

Learn more at jcapitalpropertygroup.com/resources/education

Website-only secondary CTA:

If you'd like to discuss your specific situation, visit our property discussion form.

Do not speak the secondary CTA in the video narration.

## Avoid

- We Buy Houses
- cash offer
- guaranteed offer
- urgency language
- fake testimonials
- fake reviews
- fake ratings
- unverified property facts
- legal, tax, financial, title, valuation, or repair advice

Do not publish the MP4 until the Canva source has passed text, logo, brand, and prohibited-language review.
