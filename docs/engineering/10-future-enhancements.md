# Future Enhancements

This backlog captures improvements that should be considered after foundational governance is stable.

## Near-Term

- Enterprise Professional Workforce Platform: consolidate existing workforce/property/acquisition capabilities into one governed Acquisition Decision Brief before adding roles or property connectors. Priority: high after UEIP Preview closeout.

- Universal Enterprise Integration Platform foundation: certify existing connectors behind one manifest, semantic capability, tenant, policy, gateway, health, and audit contract. Priority: high.

- Resolve ESLint silent timeout. Priority: high.
- Investigate existing Turbopack tracing warning involving `next.config.ts`, Prisma, referrals, and `app/api/leads/route.ts`. Priority: medium.
- Daily work order engine connected to AI workforce readiness.
- Sprint decision gate surfaced in dashboard or checklist form.
- Technical debt dashboard derived from sprint records.
- Connector readiness timeline with owner and safe next action.
- Production readiness scorecard.
- Sprint 12 Revenue Intelligence should improve opportunity detection and recommendation quality only; execution authority requires a separate governance gate.

## Medium-Term

- Sprint 16 Multi-Business Enablement: tenant-specific connector installation, module configuration, data boundaries, quotas, and delegated administration.
- Sprint 17 Controlled External Execution: exact-action authorization, approval binding, idempotency, compensation, audit evidence, and emergency suspension.
- Sprint 18 reconciliation: register existing read-only business connections as UEIP plug-ins while preserving implementation history.

- Persistent work order model tied to department, AI employee, approval, outcome, memory, and KPI.
- Engineering decision record generator.
- Documentation index validator.
- Connector policy simulator for read-only vs exact-action execution.
- Connector SDK, fixture harness, certification suite, and unified portfolio service-level dashboard.
- Release checklist automation.

## Long-Term

- Role-based engineering permissions.
- Architecture drift detection.
- AI employee performance scorecards.
- Memory-driven sprint recommendations.
- Governed production automation with kill switches, audit, and rollback.

## Postpone Rule

Enhancements should be postponed when they do not clearly improve revenue generation, operating cost reduction, safety, architecture quality, or the AI company operating loop.
