# Enterprise Professional Operating Company Kickoff

## Decision

J Capital Property Group may begin a supervised, internal-only operating-company pilot. The pilot optimizes qualified seller-opportunity decision quality per CEO review hour and composes the existing Executive Intelligence, Department OS, EPC, Property Intelligence, Revenue, Approval/Safety, Business Module, and UEIP boundaries.

The operating chain is:

`Business Outcome -> Executive Intake -> Department Mandates -> Professional Analysis -> Evidence -> Independent QA -> Executive Synthesis -> CEO Decision -> Verified Outcome -> Promotion or Remediation`

## Canonical Authority

The Enterprise Professional Competency Library is the canonical professional definition registry. Property Intelligence contracts remain compatible; the Enterprise Professional Operating System supplies department instantiations and portfolio composition. AI Workforce owns identities, Department OS owns missions, UEIP owns provider authority, Approval/Safety owns execution decisions, and Business Modules own industry behavior.

## Company Case

`CompanyOutcomeCaseV1` is an immutable, tenant-scoped snapshot shared across departments. It requires lead source attribution, evidence provenance, an observation cutoff, accountable professionals, independent reviewers, visible assumptions/conflicts/gaps, and an append-only audit timeline.

Property, Revenue, Marketing, and Finance may analyze the same snapshot in parallel. Creative remains blocked until an approved internal Marketing or Revenue brief exists. Uncertified departments may contribute only in supervised calibration; their work is not executive-use eligible.

## Revenue Vertical Slice

Revenue Operations is the first post-Property domain runtime. `RevenuePipelineDecisionBriefV1` deterministically exposes pipeline freshness, exceptions, stalled opportunities, buyer-demand alignment, delay factors, missing evidence, and one responsible human next decision. It cannot create tasks, mutate CRM, contact leads, activate connectors, or execute workflows.

## Executive Synthesis

`CompanyDecisionPacketV1` preserves department findings and disagreements. It recommends only a manual CEO decision: approve an internal step, request evidence, remediate, defer, prioritize connector intake, or request a separate governed execution proposal.

## Pilot Gate

The pilot requires 10 calibration and 20 blind-validation cases, at least 25% median CEO review-time improvement, at least 80% useful-or-better ratings, zero invented facts/cross-property leakage/unauthorized actions, complete seeded-critical-defect detection, no false-priority increase, complete source attribution and material-claim provenance, visible assumptions/conflicts/gaps, and a responsible human owner.

Passing evidence never promotes automatically. Promotion remains Property Intelligence, Revenue Operations, Marketing Intelligence, Finance and Executive Analytics, then Creative Studio.

## Provider Boundary

No new connector is required for kickoff. Search Console remains the UEIP runtime reference. Professional capability qualification requires an active exact-scope competency certification and a registered UEIP capability; planned capabilities cannot be qualified or invoked.
