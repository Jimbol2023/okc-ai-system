# Enterprise Professional Workforce Platform — Property Intelligence Pilot

## Decision

The first professional-workforce vertical slice optimizes acquisition decision quality per CEO research hour. It extends the existing AI Workforce, Department OS, property records, county capability, buyer-demand, acquisition, UEIP, and safety systems. It does not create a parallel workforce or authorize external action.

The first deliverable is `AcquisitionDecisionBriefV1`. It is assembled deterministically from stored evidence, exposes assumptions and missing information, runs independent QA, and remains non-persistent and advisory.

## Operating Cell

- Chief Property Intelligence Officer AI.
- Property Records and GIS Analyst AI.
- Market and Valuation Analyst AI.
- Investment and Acquisition Analyst AI.
- Property Intelligence Quality Reviewer AI.

Profiles are governed capability contracts, not autonomous agents or claims of human licensure. Certification means internal advisory readiness only.

## Evidence and Report Boundary

- A canonical property identity is required before evidence can be admitted.
- Cross-property evidence is rejected and recorded as a conflict.
- Seller-reported information remains distinct from verified facts.
- Operator-entered ARV, repairs, and profit values remain assumptions.
- Unavailable public, tax, GIS, zoning, flood, comparable, and buyer-demand evidence remains visible as a data gap.
- Readiness factors explain coverage; they are not an offer, valuation, appraisal, or execution score.
- QA cannot be performed by a report-generating profile.
- The report cannot contact, approve, create work, mutate CRM, order data, activate connectors, publish, or execute.

## ROI Gate

The calibration cohort requires at least ten leads and the blind validation cohort at least twenty. Promotion requires zero invented facts, cross-property leakage, and unauthorized actions; complete seeded-issue detection; at least 25% median research-time improvement; at least 80% useful-or-better ratings; and no increase in false high-priority recommendations.

No pilot records are invented or automatically persisted by this implementation. CEO-selected cohorts and outcomes require a separate governed operating step.

## Source Expansion

Existing and manually verified data are used first. Candidate sources receive a marginal-value score based on acquisition impact, coverage, gaps, authority, freshness, reuse, cost, licensing, and privacy. A favorable score prepares UEIP intake only; it does not activate a connector.

Recommended source order remains assessor/parcel, county GIS, FEMA, USPS, county tax/zoning, Census, Maps/routing, and finally one licensed comparable source. Marketing and creative connectors remain outside the Property Intelligence evidence path.

## Validation

- Professional profile and certification invariants.
- Canonical identity and cross-property rejection.
- Evidence provenance, assumptions, conflicts, and missing-data visibility.
- Deterministic brief assembly and independent QA.
- Appraisal, Fair Housing, legal/title/tax/zoning/flood, outreach, offer, CRM, provider, and execution boundaries.
- Pilot ROI and marginal-source gates.
- Mobile-first executive rendering and authenticated read-only API visibility.
