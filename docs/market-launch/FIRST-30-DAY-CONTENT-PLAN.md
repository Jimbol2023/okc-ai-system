# First 30-Day Content Plan

## Purpose

Provide manual content ideas for the first 30 days after profile launch. This plan does not authorize AI posting, automated publishing, campaigns, outreach, CRM activity, or messaging automation.

## Content Rules

- Keep content educational and no-pressure.
- Do not invent property facts, reviews, ratings, statistics, or market claims.
- Avoid urgent language, money graphics, and aggressive acquisition language.
- Use accessible captions, plain language, and readable visuals.
- Link back to https://jcapitalpropertygroup.com where appropriate.

## Manual Publishing Checklist

- Review each post before publishing.
- Confirm no private property facts are included.
- Confirm no promises or unsupported claims are included.
- Add alt text to images where the platform supports it.
- Keep comments and messages manually reviewed by the operator.
