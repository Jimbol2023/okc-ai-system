# Oklahoma Content Foundation

## Purpose

Plan a conservative content architecture for future organic search growth. This document is planning only and does not create a content library.

## Target Audience

Oklahoma property owners who want clear, professional guidance about property situations, ownership decisions, maintenance concerns, landlord fatigue, relocation, vacancy, or inherited property.

## Future Page Or Article Candidates

- How To Handle An Inherited Property In Oklahoma
- Options For Vacant Property Owners In Oklahoma
- Property Guidance For Landlords In Oklahoma
- Understanding Property Solutions In Oklahoma
- When To Discuss A Property Situation With A Professional

## Content Purpose

- Explain common property situations in plain language.
- Help owners understand questions to consider before deciding next steps.
- Reinforce J Capital Property Group as professional, transparent, local, and no-pressure.
- Avoid promises, pressure, or unsupported claims.

## Internal Linking Strategy

- Link informational topics to `/sell-your-house` when the reader may want a private conversation.
- Link company trust and values references to `/about`.
- Link phone and email calls to action to `/contact`.
- Link privacy expectations to `/privacy` where relevant.

## Compliance And Safety Language Rules

- Do not use aggressive investor slogans.
- Do not promise immediate purchase, payment, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Do not invent statistics, testimonials, reviews, ratings, or business metrics.
- Do not use urgency tactics, countdowns, dollar signs, or cash graphics.
- Keep property guidance general unless facts are verified.

## No-Pressure Positioning

Future content should consistently state that property owners can ask questions, compare possible next steps, and decide what makes sense for their situation.

## Service-Area Expansion Notes

- Start with Oklahoma-wide framing.
- Add city or county pages only when there is a clear operational reason and accurate local knowledge.
- Do not publish location pages with thin, duplicated, or unsupported claims.
