# Daily Operating Cycle

The AI workforce foundation supports a daily internal operating rhythm.

## Morning

- Daily Briefing Analyst AI reviews read-only/internal signals.
- CEO Executive Assistant AI prepares the decision brief.
- Connector Health Monitor AI surfaces tool and connector gaps.
- Company Orchestrator AI prepares department assignments.

## Midday

- Department managers review assigned internal work.
- Employees produce drafts, checklists, summaries, and blocker notes.
- Approval Gatekeeper AI routes risky or external-facing items to CEO review.

## End Of Day

- CRM, Finance, Operations, and Knowledge / Memory summarize outcomes.
- Lessons Learned Analyst AI turns outcomes and blockers into next-day inputs.
- No external action is performed unless a future governed execution policy exists.

## Sprint 0 Definition Of Done

The system can answer:

- What AI departments exist?
- Who manages each department?
- Which AI employees work there?
- What each employee does daily?
- Which tools each employee needs?
- Which tools are ready, missing, idle, or blocked?
- Which employees can produce internal-only output today?
- Which employees are blocked and why?
- Which KPI or revenue goal each employee supports?
- Which actions require CEO approval?
- Whether external execution remains prevented without approval.
