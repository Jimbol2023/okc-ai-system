# J Capital AI Company Vision

J Capital Property Group is building an AI-first operating company where departments, managers, and AI employees prepare daily internal work toward revenue generation.

Sprint 0 establishes the workforce foundation. It defines who owns work, which manager assigned it, which AI employee is responsible, what tools are needed, what KPI is affected, what output should be produced, and what blocks the work.

## Operating Principles

- AI employees prepare internal work only unless a future governed policy authorizes exact external execution.
- Every recommendation must keep source labels, assumptions, blockers, and approval requirements visible.
- No AI employee may send, publish, post, scrape, text, email, schedule, call external providers, create ads, or mutate external systems in Sprint 0.
- Connector readiness is not permission to execute.
- CEO approval is required before any future public, seller-facing, spend-related, or external-provider action.

## Business Outcome

The workforce foundation should make the daily company question answerable:

What should J Capital work on today, who owns it, what is blocked, and what can safely be produced internally?
