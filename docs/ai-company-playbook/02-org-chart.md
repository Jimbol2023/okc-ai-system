# AI Org Chart

The AI workforce is organized around operating departments, not isolated tools.

## Divisions

- Executive: CEO Office, AI COO
- Revenue: Lead Generation, Seller Acquisition, CRM, Acquisitions
- Growth: Marketing, Design, Content, SEO, Social Media
- Property Intelligence: County Intelligence
- Operations: Finance, Operations
- AI Core: Knowledge / Memory
- Governance: Approval / Safety

## Department Manager Defaults

- CEO Office: CEO Executive Assistant AI
- AI COO: Company Orchestrator AI
- Lead Generation: Lead Research Analyst AI
- Seller Acquisition: Seller Lead Prioritization AI
- CRM: CRM Manager AI
- Marketing: Marketing Director AI
- Design: Creative Director AI
- Content: Content Director AI
- SEO: SEO Director AI
- Social Media: Social Media Manager AI
- County Intelligence: County Records Analyst AI
- Acquisitions: Deal Analyst AI
- Finance: Revenue Analyst AI
- Operations: Connector Health Monitor AI
- Knowledge / Memory: Memory Curator AI
- Approval / Safety: Approval Gatekeeper AI

## Handoff Rule

Departments do not independently execute external actions. They hand work to the AI COO, Approval / Safety, or the CEO Office when approval, compliance, public output, seller contact, spend, or provider execution is involved.
