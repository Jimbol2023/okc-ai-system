## Business Description

J Capital Property Group provides professional, no-pressure property guidance for Oklahoma property owners. The company helps owners organize practical questions around inherited property, vacant homes, landlord property decisions, relocation property, deferred maintenance, and private property discussions. Conversations are educational and trust-first, with encouragement to speak with qualified legal, tax, title, insurance, valuation, or repair professionals when those topics apply.

## Services

- **Inherited Property Guidance**  
  Educational property conversations for Oklahoma owners reviewing inherited property documents, family communication, occupancy, condition, timing, and possible next steps.

- **Vacant Property Guidance**  
  Practical guidance for owners thinking through vacant property access, monitoring, utilities, insurance, taxes, repairs, and timing.

- **Probate-Related Property Questions**  
  General property education for owners organizing probate-related property questions. This is not legal advice, and owners should speak with qualified professionals.

- **Landlord Property Decisions**  
  Property conversations for landlords reviewing rental goals, repairs, management responsibilities, tenant-related considerations, and changing priorities.

- **Relocation Property Decisions**  
  Guidance for owners managing Oklahoma property questions while moving or living outside the immediate area.

- **Private Property Discussion**  
  A direct, respectful conversation for owners who want to organize property facts, timing, condition, and next-step questions before deciding what makes sense.

## GBP Q&A

**Q: Does J Capital Property Group work with inherited properties?**  
A: J Capital Property Group can discuss practical inherited property questions such as documents, timing, condition, family communication, occupancy, and next steps. Legal, tax, title, and probate questions should be reviewed with qualified professionals.

**Q: Can I contact J Capital about a vacant property?**  
A: Yes. Owners can discuss practical questions such as access, utilities, insurance, taxes, known repairs, monitoring, and timing for a vacant Oklahoma property.

**Q: Does J Capital Property Group provide probate advice?**  
A: No. J Capital Property Group provides general property education and practical property conversations. Probate, legal authority, title, and estate questions should be directed to qualified professionals.

**Q: Can out-of-state owners contact J Capital Property Group?**  
A: Yes. Out-of-state owners can organize questions about Oklahoma property access, documents, occupancy, condition, local responsibilities, and possible next steps.

**Q: Is there any obligation when I contact J Capital Property Group?**  
A: No. The first step can be a simple, no-pressure conversation about the property situation, timing, condition, and owner goals.

## Week 1 GBP Post Calendar

1. **Understanding Inherited Property in Oklahoma**  
   Inherited property decisions can involve documents, family communication, condition, timing, and professional guidance. Start by organizing the situation before making major decisions.

2. **Do Not Rush Inherited Property Decisions**  
   Families often benefit from slowing down, gathering facts, and confirming who has authority before deciding whether to keep, rent, repair, or sell a property.

3. **Gather Important Documents First**  
   Useful documents may include estate documents, title information, tax records, insurance details, utility information, mortgage records, repair notes, and occupancy details.

4. **Review Taxes, Insurance, Utilities, and Repairs**  
   Property responsibilities can continue while decisions are being made. Review taxes, insurance, utility status, access, maintenance, and known repair needs.

5. **Speak With Qualified Professionals**  
   Some property decisions involve legal, tax, title, valuation, insurance, or repair questions. Qualified professionals can help clarify situation-specific concerns.
