# Professional Toolchains

## Decision

J Capital AI OS organizes provider capabilities around governed professionals and business deliverables, not around integrations. The canonical operating chain is:

`CEO Objective -> AI COO Case -> Department Mandate -> Professional Assignment -> Expertise + Toolchain -> Evidence -> Deliverable -> Independent QA -> CEO Decision -> Approved Action -> Verified Outcome -> Institutional Learning`

Professional Toolchains extend the Enterprise Professional Competency Library. They do not replace AI Workforce identity, Department OS missions, UEIP provider authority, Approval / Safety execution decisions, or Business Module ownership of industry behavior.

## Contracts

- `ProfessionalExpertisePackV1` defines versioned enterprise, functional, industry, or regional expertise, approved source hierarchy, assessment cases, prohibited conclusions, freshness, and recertification triggers.
- `ProfessionalToolchainContractV1` binds an accountable professional, independent reviewer, expertise, competencies, SOPs, exact connector capabilities, governed deliverables, controls, fallbacks, and measurable business value.
- `CertificationWaveContractV1` defines professionals, toolchains, deliverables, prerequisites, proof thresholds, blockers, and the required human promotion decision.
- `ConnectorIntakeDecisionV1` explains why a capability is ineligible, needs a business case, certification, registration, security review, calibration, or is ready for a separate governed enablement proposal.

Definitions remain code-versioned. Assessments, certifications, qualifications, outcomes, and governance decisions remain append-only tenant-scoped EPC records.

## Connector Intake Constitution

No connector capability may be onboarded unless a named professional is certified for its required competency, qualified for the exact capability, accountable for a governed deliverable it measurably improves, and subject to independent QA.

Intake also requires normalized evidence, least privilege, credential ownership, privacy and licensing review, audit coverage, rate-limit behavior, feature flags, a kill switch, safe fallback, and measurable success and retirement criteria.

Intake readiness never activates a connector. Certification never grants provider authority. Connector enablement never grants external execution.

## First Release

Wave 0 activates the Professional Standards, QA, evidence, learning, and intake foundations. Wave 1 activates Search and Market Intelligence:

- Senior SEO Director — Search Console — Executive SEO Brief.
- Senior Analytics Specialist — Search Console plus staged GA4 — Content Opportunity Report.
- Local Visibility Specialist — staged Google Business Profile — Local Visibility Report.
- Independent Marketing Quality Reviewer — blocks unsupported, self-reviewed, or authority-violating work.

Search Console is the only live provider path and remains governed Preview read-only through UEIP. GA4 and Google Business Profile use stored or manual normalized evidence; missing evidence becomes a visible data gap. Report assembly performs no provider calls or external writes.

## Proof And Promotion

Every wave requires at least 10 calibration cases, 20 blind-validation cases, zero invented facts and unauthorized actions, complete seeded-critical-defect detection, at least 25% median time improvement, at least 80% useful-or-better ratings, no harmful-error increase, and a human promotion decision.

Learning may recommend a versioned expertise, competency, SOP, toolchain, or report change. It cannot apply the change or expand authority autonomously.
