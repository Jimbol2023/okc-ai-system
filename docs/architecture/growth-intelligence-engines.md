# Growth Intelligence Engines

## Purpose

Growth Intelligence Engines turn approved company activity, founder expertise, relationships, and market signals into high-ROI internal recommendations, drafts, briefs, and review queues. They extend the Revenue Spine, Marketing Hub, Tool Registry, and Safe Auto Mode without authorizing external execution.

## Personal Brand Engine

### Scope

- Founder profile intake and approval.
- Approved source intake for deals, projects, market knowledge, AI automation improvements, milestones, FAQs, team achievements, client stories, and executive insights.
- Draft-only content for LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok, Google Business Profile, company website, personal website, podcasts, publications, press, newsletters, events, speaking, webinars, and courses.
- Repurposing from one approved source into blog, social, newsletter, podcast, webinar, video, infographic, guide, and lead magnet formats.

### Safety

- No founder fact, credential, client story, deal result, property fact, revenue claim, or market statistic may be invented.
- Client success stories require approval and default to anonymized language.
- Publishing remains blocked.

## Network & Relationship Engine

### Scope

- Relationship graph for sellers, buyers, lenders, investors, brokers, wholesalers, contractors, property managers, attorneys, title companies, mortgage professionals, insurance professionals, CPAs, wealth advisors, financial institutions, AI/technology partners, agencies, community organizations, local businesses, influencers, podcast hosts, journalists, conference organizers, universities, economic development groups, acquisition partners, JV partners, and future franchise partners.
- Advisory scoring for relationship health, opportunity, trust, partnership potential, lifetime value, and follow-up priority.
- Meeting briefs, follow-up drafts, referral intelligence, partnership intelligence, and business development review queues.

### Safety

- The engine may discover, organize, analyze, draft, recommend, prioritize, schedule internally, and monitor.
- It must not send messages, accept invitations, commit to partnerships, negotiate agreements, submit proposals, schedule meetings externally, share confidential information, or publish communications without approval.

## Macro Intelligence Layer

### Scope

- Approved-source registry for local/national news, economic indicators, policy updates, interest rates, housing policy, lending conditions, tax changes, zoning, infrastructure, employment trends, and industry publications.
- Manual/import-ready analysis for market opportunities, risk alerts, hot ZIP candidates, buyer/seller motivation shifts, and investment timing signals.

### Safety

- Live news APIs, paid data providers, RSS ingestion, and third-party connectors remain readiness-only until approved.
- Every macro signal requires attribution, confidence, affected market, and missing-data notes.

## Shared Governance

| Control | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Tool usage | Route through Tool Registry. |
| Automation | Evaluate through Safe Auto Mode. |
| Source facts | Require provenance and assumptions. |
| External actions | Require approval. |
| Audit | Record decision, evidence, risk, and safety flags. |
| Testing | Prove no provider calls, sends, publishes, schedules, or scraping in v1. |
